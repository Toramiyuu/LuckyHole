Our Story

Honest Food, Good Times

Lucky Hole is the vision of Chef Waymann Cheong — a converted warehouse on Beach Street where bold flavours meet an industrial-chic setting. What started in October 2023 has quickly become one of George Town's most talked-about dining spots.

Every dish is cooked over charcoal on the open grill, blending French technique with Chinese and Japanese influences while staying rooted in Penang. The menu changes every three months, driven by seasonal ingredients and creative freedom.

We make honest, feel-good food that we truly love — nothing fancy, just fun and easy-going like us. Come as you are.
